RanksRangos

Level 1 to 50 in each playlist. Only wins and losses count, not kills. Beating someone higher gets you more, the first levels go fast, and from 42 up each win is worth less. Quitting a match is a loss.Nivel 1 a 50 en cada lista. Solo cuentan las victorias y derrotas, no las bajas.
