NOV 10, 202610 NOV 2026v0.18.667, v0.18.668

What's newNovedades

  • The launcher's menus are lighter to draw on every PC.
  • The log now records how smoothly the main menu and its background video run, minute by minute when they slow down. It helps us track down the slow main menu some Linux players reported: if it happens to you, please send us your h2gravemind.log.

  • El registro ahora anota qué tan fluidos van el menú principal y su video de fondo, minuto a minuto cuando se ponen lentos. Nos ayuda a encontrar el menú principal lento que reportaron algunos jugadores de Linux: si te pasa, envíanos tu h2gravemind.log.

Bug fixesCorrecciones

  • Linux / Steam Deck (Proton, Wine): the main menu no longer slows down the longer it stays open. It used to drop from over 100 frames a second to 30-65 within a few minutes.
